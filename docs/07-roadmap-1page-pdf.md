## Why this scale of Azure investment is justified

1. **Real users today** — 189 active warga in pilot RT 003 Purwokerto, demonstrated weekly engagement
2. **Validated pull** — 5+ neighboring RTs already on waitlist for Q1 onboarding
3. **Pak RW endorsement** — verbal commitment to recommend platform across 10 RTs under one RW
4. **Government tailwind** — Indonesia's Dana Desa allocates ~Rp 60M/year per village for IT digitalization (83K villages × Rp 60M = Rp 5T addressable)
5. **WhatsApp habit** — 90%+ of Indonesian adults use WhatsApp daily; we ride existing distribution
6. **No dominant incumbent** — Government-built eRT/RW has near-zero adoption; private competitors focus on enterprise B2G, not RT-level

## Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Pak RT adoption resistance | WhatsApp-first UX (no app install for warga); hands-on onboarding; printed manuals |
| Data privacy concerns (UU PDP) | Azure-native encryption; per-role access; audit logging via Sentinel; Indonesian data residency |
| Azure cost overrun | Cost alerts at 50/75/90%; serverless-first; scale-to-zero on idle; Reserved Instances |
| Solo founder bandwidth | Hire 1 part-time CSM by Q2; co-founder search initiated Q1 |
| Slow B2G sales cycle | Bottom-up adoption first (RT → RW → Kelurahan → Pemkot); not blocked on tender process |
